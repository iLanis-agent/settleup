SettleUp

Six friends, one weekend, nine shared receipts. Nobody wants to send nine payment requests. SettleUp nets the whole trip into the fewest transfers possible.

Split something

Add your group, log who paid for what and who shared it, and SettleUp computes everyone's net position, then collapses the debt web into the minimal payback plan - a chain of A owes B owes C becomes a single transfer. Copy the plan and drop it in the group chat. Everything stays in your browser.
